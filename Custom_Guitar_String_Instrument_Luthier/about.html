The FRET Story

Dedicated to the art of handcrafted string instruments.

Heritage

Rooted in Tradition. Built for Tomorrow.

Founded in 1988, FRET was born from a singular belief: that an instrument should be as inspiring to hold as it is to hear.

When our founder opened the first iteration of this workshop in a small garage in Portland, the goal was simple: to build a better acoustic guitar. Dissatisfied with the thick finishes and overbuilt bracing of mass-produced instruments of the era, the focus turned back to the golden age of lutherie.

By studying vintage dreadnoughts and pre-war parlor guitars, we reverse-engineered the techniques that made those instruments legendary. We rediscovered the importance of hot hide glue, not just for its reversibility, but for its crystalline hardness that transfers resonance perfectly between two pieces of wood.

Over the decades, our small team has grown, and our catalog has expanded to include electric models that employ the same obsessive attention to detail. Yet, our core philosophy remains unchanged. We don't use CNC machines to carve our necks, because a machine cannot feel the grain. We don't rush our finishing process, because nitrocellulose lacquer needs time to cure and breathe.

Today, a FRET instrument is the culmination of thousands of small, deliberate decisions. From the ethical sourcing of our master grade tonewoods to the final setup on the bench, every step is executed by a master craftsman. We build guitars not just for the players of today, but to be heirloom pieces passed down for generations.
