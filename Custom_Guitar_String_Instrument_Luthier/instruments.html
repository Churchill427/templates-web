Instrument Care

Keep your instrument between 45-55% relative humidity and away from direct heat sources. Extreme dryness causes cracks; extreme moisture causes swelling.
Wipe down the neck and strings after every use with a dry microfiber cloth. Avoid silicone-based polishes on our nitrocellulose finishes.
If traveling by air or storing the instrument for months, tune down one whole step to relieve tension on the neck and bridge.
