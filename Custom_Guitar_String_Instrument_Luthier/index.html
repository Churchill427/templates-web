Our Story

The Luthier's Bench

Founded in 1988, FRET was born from a singular belief: that an instrument should be as inspiring to hold as it is to hear.

We don't use CNC machines for our neck profiles. We carve them by hand, feeling the grain, reading the wood, and ensuring that the final shape rests perfectly in the player's palm. It is a slow, methodical process that cannot be rushed.
