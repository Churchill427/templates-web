Our Methodology

The Restoration Process

1. Assessment & Documentation

Every instrument is photographed, measured, and internally scoped. We assess structural integrity, originality, and current playability before proposing a plan.

2. Stabilize

Immediate threats to the instrument are addressed first. Loose braces are glued, cracks are cleated, and neck joints are secured using traditional hot hide glue.

3. Repair & Rebuild

With the structure sound, we move to functionality. This includes refrets, bridge reglues, or fabricating missing components from period-correct materials.

4. Refinish & Blend

We never over-restore. Repairs are seamlessly blended into the existing patina using tinted lacquers or French polish, preserving the instrument's history.

5. Final Setup

The instrument is strung, settled, and dialed in. Action, relief, and intonation are optimized to the player's specific preferences.
