About PRNT

Our Story

Born in a Garage, Built in Ink

PRNT started in 2015 with one manual press, a squeegee, and a dream to make custom apparel accessible to every brand — big or small. What began as late-night print runs for local bands quickly grew into Portland's go-to screen printing studio.

In those early days, our founder Alex Martinez would personally burn screens, mix inks by hand, and cure shirts with a small flash dryer until 3 AM. The dedication to getting every detail right—even on small runs—built a loyal local following that spread by word of mouth.

Today, we operate 12 automatic presses, a dedicated embroidery department, and a team of 25 passionate printmakers. We've served over 340 brands and printed more than 125,000 garments — and we're just getting started.

Our philosophy remains exactly the same as day one: treat every order like it's our own merch. That means obsessing over color accuracy, inspecting every piece, and delivering on time — every time. Whether you're a startup clothing brand or a Fortune 500 company, you get the same level of obsessive craftsmanship.

Eco-Friendly Printing

Our Commitment to Sustainability

The apparel industry has historically had a heavy environmental footprint. At PRNT, we are actively working to change that narrative through conscious choices on our production floor.

  • Water-Based Inks & Cleaners

    We prioritize 100% water-based and phthalate-free discharge inks, and exclusively use soy and citrus-based screen cleaners that are non-toxic to our local waterways.

  • Zero-Waste Packaging

    All our outgoing shipments utilize recycled cardboard boxes, compostable mailers, and paper tape. We recycle 95% of incoming supplier packaging.

  • Sustainable Blanks Sourcing

    We partner with manufacturers who hold WRAP, OEKO-TEX, and Fair Trade certifications to ensure ethical labor and organic material sourcing.
