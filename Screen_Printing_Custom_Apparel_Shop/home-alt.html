Our Techniques

Ink & Print Methods

Every method mastered for the perfect finish

Plastisol Ink

Industry standard — vibrant, opaque, durable on dark fabrics. Perfect for bold designs.

Water-Based Ink

Soft hand feel that breathes — eco-friendly with a vintage, worn-in look.

Discharge Print

Removes garment dye and replaces with color — no ink layer, ultra-soft feel.

DTG Printing

Direct-to-garment for photo-quality detail — ideal for complex multi-color artwork.

Heat Transfer

Vinyl cut & pressed — great for names, numbers, and small batch custom pieces.
