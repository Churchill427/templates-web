Vector vs. Raster

Vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) are created using mathematical formulas, meaning they can be scaled infinitely without losing quality. They are the absolute best format for screen printing because they allow us to easily separate colors for the different screens.

Raster files (PNG, PSD, JPEG) are made of pixels. If you must use a raster file, it must be created at actual print size with a minimum resolution of 300 DPI. A low-resolution image pulled from the web will result in a blurry, pixelated print.

Pantone Color Matching

Monitors display colors in RGB (Light), but we print using physical ink. What looks neon green on your screen might look duller on a shirt unless properly color-matched.

For exact color replication, we use the Solid Coated Pantone (PMS) Matching System. If you provide PMS color codes with your artwork, our ink technicians will custom mix the ink to match those exact hues with 98% accuracy.

Best Practices

Do's & Don'ts

Do's

  • Use vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) for sharpest results
  • Convert text to outlines/paths before exporting
  • Provide Pantone (PMS) color references
  • Export PNG at 300+ DPI with transparent background
  • Include bleed area for edge-to-edge prints
  • Test contrast of ink vs. garment color

Don'ts

  • Don't submit low-res JPEGs or screenshots
  • Don't use RGB colors (they shift in print)
  • Don't include white backgrounds on dark garments
  • Don't use fine hairline strokes (min 1pt)
  • Don't embed ICC profiles without noting them
  • Don't submit copyrighted artwork without rights
Troubleshooting

Why Designs Get Rejected

Avoid these common pitfalls to ensure your file goes straight to production.

Fonts Not Outlined

If you use a custom font but don't outline it, our computers might replace it with a default font like Arial. Always right-click your text and select "Create Outlines" before saving your vector file.

Tiny Details & Lines

Screen printing uses physical mesh screens. Lines thinner than 1pt or extremely small text may not burn properly into the screen or might get clogged with ink during the print run.

Hidden Backgrounds

Submitting a design with a white background block when you want it printed on a black shirt means we will print that white box. Always export PNGs with a transparent background.
