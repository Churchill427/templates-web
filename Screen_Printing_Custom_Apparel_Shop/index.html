Our Process

How It Works

From upload to delivery in 4 simple steps

Upload

Upload your artwork in any standard format (AI, PSD, PNG). Our team reviews every file for resolution and color separation to guarantee perfect prints.

Proof

Receive a high-fidelity digital mockup showing exact placement, scale, and Pantone ink colors. Nothing goes to press without your final sign-off.

Print

Your garments hit our automated M&R presses. We meticulously monitor ink deposits and curing temperatures for vibrant, durable results.

Deliver

Every piece passes a strict QC check before being neatly folded, boxed, and shipped. Track your order door-to-door or pick it up locally.

Capabilities

Beyond Screen Printing

Comprehensive apparel solutions for professional brands

Custom Relabeling

Build true brand equity. We carefully remove manufacturer tags and screen-print or sew your custom neck labels directly into the garment.

Retail Finishing

Get shelf-ready products. We offer individual polybagging, barcode sticker application, hang-tagging, and professional folding services.

Fulfillment & Warehousing

Don't want to store 5,000 shirts in your office? We warehouse your inventory and ship directly to your customers or retail locations on demand.
