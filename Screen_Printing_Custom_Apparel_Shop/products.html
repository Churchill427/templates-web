Quality Assurance

Why Choose Our Blanks?

We don't print on cheap shirts. Here's why our apparel stands out.

Retail-Ready Fit

Say goodbye to boxy, ill-fitting promo tees. Our garments feature side-seam construction and tailored fits that people actually want to wear.

Wash Durability

Pre-shrunk fabrics and tightly woven yarns ensure that both the garment and the print survive hundreds of trips through the laundry.

Optimal Print Surface

We select fabrics with a high stitch density and smooth surface, allowing our inks to lay flat for sharper details and brighter colors.

Try Before You Buy

Ordering Samples

Yes! We highly encourage ordering blank samples if you are unsure about sizing, color, or fabric weight. Blank samples are charged at the single-unit retail price.
Due to the extensive setup time required for screen printing, we do not offer one-off printed samples. However, we provide high-fidelity digital mockups for approval, and for orders over 500 units, we can schedule a press-check.
If you proceed with a bulk order of 100+ units of that exact garment style, we will credit the cost of up to 3 blank samples towards your final invoice.
