The Experience

From the moment you enter to the final reveal, every second is designed to thrill.

Step 1

The Briefing

Your game master sets the scene, explains the rules, and hands you the first clue. The clock hasn't started yet — but your pulse already has.

Step 2

The Lock-In

The door seals. The lights shift. You're inside the narrative now — every object, sound, and shadow is a potential key to freedom.

Step 3

The Countdown

Adrenaline peaks as time ticks away. Combine logic, teamwork, and instinct to crack the final sequence before the buzzer sounds.

Step 4

The Debrief

Win or learn, the debrief reveals the secrets you missed, celebrates your triumphs, and captures a victory photo for the leaderboard.
