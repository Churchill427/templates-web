About MYST

The minds, the mission, and the mystery behind every room.

Our Story

MYST was born in 2018 from a simple obsession: what if a puzzle could consume every one of your senses? Our founders — a theatrical set designer, a cryptographer, and a game developer — pooled their crafts to build immersive worlds where stories aren't told, they're lived.

From a single room in a converted warehouse, we've grown to five meticulously themed chambers, each taking over 2,000 hours to conceive, design, and build. Every lock has a narrative reason. Every hidden compartment rewards curiosity.

Today, MYST is ranked among the top 10 escape room venues in the country, with over 48,000 players who've stepped through our doors — and stepped out changed.

Mission & Values

The principles that shape every experience we create.

Immersion Above All

Every detail — from lighting and sound design to tactile props — is crafted to dissolve the boundary between game and reality.

Inclusive by Design

Our rooms welcome all ages, abilities, and experience levels. We believe everyone deserves the thrill of a breakthrough moment.

Relentless Innovation

We retire and rebuild rooms annually. New mechanics, new stories, new technology — stagnation is the only puzzle we refuse to solve.
