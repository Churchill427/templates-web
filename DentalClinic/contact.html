Common Questions

FAQ

You can book an appointment online through our Booking page, call us at +1 (555) DENT-001, or visit our clinic in person. Online bookings receive confirmation within 24 hours.

We accept most major dental insurance plans including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, and United Healthcare. Contact us to verify your specific coverage before your visit.

For dental emergencies, call our 24/7 emergency line at +1 (555) DENT-911. We prioritize emergency cases and can often see you within the hour during business hours.

Please bring a valid photo ID, your dental insurance card, a list of current medications, and any recent dental X-rays. Arrive 15 minutes early to complete paperwork.

We accept cash, all major credit/debit cards, HSA/FSA cards, and offer flexible financing through CareCredit. Payment plans are available for treatments over $500.
