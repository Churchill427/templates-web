Be Ready

Prepare for Your Visit

  • Bring a valid photo ID (driver's license, passport, or state ID)
  • Bring your dental insurance card and member information
  • Arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time
  • Prepare a list of current medications and dosages
  • Bring recent dental X-rays if available (digital copies accepted)
  • Note any allergies, medical conditions, or dental anxiety concerns

Cancellation Policy

We understand plans change. Please provide at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations (less than 24 hours) may incur a $50 fee. No-shows may be charged the full appointment cost. To cancel or reschedule, call us at +1 (555) DENT-001 or email appointments@dentclinic.com.
