About JUMP

Built on a love of movement, community, and the belief that everyone deserves to fly.

Our Story

JUMP was born in 2018 when two former gymnasts decided their community needed a space where families could experience the pure joy of bouncing — safely, affordably, and together.

What started as a 4-court warehouse has grown into a 12,000+ sq ft arena with 9 adventure zones, serving over 150,000 jumpers every year.

"We wanted to build a place where gravity is just a suggestion and smiles are guaranteed."

Mission & Values

Joy in Motion

We believe movement brings happiness. Every zone, event, and experience is designed to spark genuine joy for every visitor.

Safety Without Compromise

From daily equipment checks to certified court marshals, safety is not negotiable — it is the foundation of everything we do.

Community First

We are more than a business — we are neighbors. Through fundraisers, accessible sessions, and local partnerships, we give back.
