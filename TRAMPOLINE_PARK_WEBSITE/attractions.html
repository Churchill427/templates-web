Foam Pit Physics of Fun

Our foam pit is engineered for maximum fun and safety. Over 10,000 foam cubes absorb your landing while the angled trampoline runway gives you the perfect launch trajectory.

Choose from three entry points: the standard trampoline launch, the climbing wall drop, or the elevated platform dive.

  • 10,000+ premium foam cubes
  • 3 entry points at different heights
  • Regular deep-cleaning and inspection
