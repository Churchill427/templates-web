Our Story

Crafting Comfort Since 2018

TINY was founded in 2018 by two parents who struggled to find baby wear that was clean, highly comfortable, and designed to move with kids' natural playing states. We set out to change things by working only with organic cotton mills.

Today, TINY is proud to clothe over 50,000 children globally, supplying school uniforms to educational centers and providing organic everyday styles that protect the sensitive skin of kids and newborns.

We test every thread count, button, and zipper frame for strength. Our garments do not feature annoying tags or scratchy interior seams. Experience the TINY comfort difference today.

Our Core Values

The beliefs that direct our daily work and textile productions

Sustainability First

We source GOTS certified cotton and utilize eco-friendly dyes to safeguard water resources and children health.

Absolute Comfort

Every apparel item features flatlock seams and tagless necklines to prevent skin scratchiness or irritation.

Heirloom Quality

Our uniforms and playsuits are double-stitched and pre-shrunk, made to be passed down through siblings.

Fair Partnerships

We verify that all weaving mills and uniform garment factories maintain ethical working wages and practices.
