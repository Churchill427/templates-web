Common Questions (FAQ)

Quick answers to frequently asked shipping and payment inquiries

What is GOTS Certified organic cotton?

GOTS is the global leading textile processing standard for organic fibers. It verifies that our materials are grown ecologically, harvested ethically, and do not contain toxic processing chemicals.

Do you accept international orders?

Yes, we ship to over 40 countries worldwide. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination.

How do I handle returns or size exchanges?

Returns and exchanges are free within a 30-day window! Make sure the garments are unworn and retain their original tag attachments, then initiate a ticket via email.

Can we request embroidery for schools?

Absolutely. Our School Uniform and Wholesale division handles customized school badge embroideries. You can submit details using our wholesale enquiry form on the Home Wholesale page.
