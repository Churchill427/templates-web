How It Works

From arrival to departure, here's exactly what happens when you bring your vehicle to TRAX.

1

Inspection & Assessment

We perform a thorough visual and digital inspection of all four tyres — checking tread depth, sidewall condition, pressure levels, and signs of uneven wear from misalignment.

  • Digital tread depth measurement
  • Sidewall damage check
  • Pressure & valve inspection
2

Tyre Fitting & Replacement

Using automated tyre changers, we remove old tyres and mount new ones with precision. Bead sealing and inflation are done to manufacturer specs to prevent leaks.

  • Automated tyre changeover
  • Torque-set lug nut tightening
  • TPMS sensor reset
3

Wheel Alignment & Balancing

Our 3D laser alignment system adjusts camber, caster, and toe to factory specifications. Computerised balancing eliminates vibrations for a perfectly smooth ride.

  • 3D laser alignment (all 4 wheels)
  • Road-force wheel balancing
  • Before/after printout provided
4

Final Check & Handover

A senior technician performs a quality check on all work completed. You receive a digital safety report, warranty documentation, and recommended next service date.

  • Quality assurance inspection
  • Digital safety report emailed
  • Service warranty activated
