Seasonal Tyre Guide

Choosing the right tyre for the season can improve braking by up to 30% and extend tyre life by thousands of miles. Here's what you need to know.

Summer

Summer Tyres

Best above 7°C / 45°F
  • Harder compound stays firm in heat — prevents excessive wear
  • Wide tread blocks maximise dry road contact for sharper steering
  • Shallow grooves channel water efficiently to resist aquaplaning
  • Lower rolling resistance improves fuel efficiency by 5–8%

Pro Tip: Switch to summer tyres in early spring once overnight temps consistently stay above 7°C. Running winter tyres in warm weather wears them 40% faster.

Shop Summer Tyres
Winter

Winter Tyres

Best below 7°C / 45°F
  • Softer silica compound stays flexible in freezing temperatures
  • Thousands of tiny sipes grip ice and packed snow like micro-claws
  • Reduces braking distance on snow by up to 30% vs. summer tyres
  • Deep V-shaped grooves expel slush and prevent snow buildup

Pro Tip: Fit winter tyres on all 4 wheels — mixing types causes unpredictable handling. Look for the 3PMSF (mountain snowflake) symbol for certified cold-weather performance.

Shop Winter Tyres
All-Season

All-Season Tyres

Effective from -5°C to 35°C
  • Moderate compound balances flexibility in cold and stability in warmth
  • Symmetrical tread pattern provides even wear for longer tyre life
  • Eliminates the cost and hassle of twice-yearly tyre swaps
  • Ideal for regions with mild winters and light rain or occasional frost

Pro Tip: All-season tyres are a great budget choice for mild climates, but if you regularly face heavy snow or icy roads, dedicated winter tyres are significantly safer.

Shop All-Season Tyres
Performance

Performance Tyres

Best above 10°C / 50°F
  • Ultra-sticky compound delivers maximum grip for aggressive cornering
  • Wider, lower-profile design offers precise steering response
  • Reinforced sidewalls handle higher speeds and lateral forces
  • Shorter braking distances at highway speeds compared.

Pro Tip: Performance tyres wear faster due to their softer compound. Check tread depth monthly and rotate every 5,000 miles to maximise lifespan and even wear.

Shop Performance Tyres
