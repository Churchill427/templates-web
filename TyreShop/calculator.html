4. Tyre Care Guide

Practical maintenance tips straight from our senior technicians to keep your vehicle running smoothly.

Rotation Guidelines

Rotating your tyres redistributes tread wear evenly across front and rear axles. We recommend a cross or front-to-back rotation every 8,000 to 10,000 km, or during your seasonal oil changes. Proper alignment during rotation ensures optimal grip and maximum longevity.

  • Fwd Vehicles: Rotate front directly back; cross rear to front
  • Rwd Vehicles: Cross front to rear; rotate rear directly forward
  • Awd Vehicles: Cross-rotate all four corners systematically

Pressure Checks

Tyres lose pressure naturally over time and with fluctuating ambient temperatures. Low pressure creates excess sidewall flexing, leading to heat buildup and catastrophic blowouts. High pressure reduces tread contact area, causing center wear and harsh ride quality.

  • Check tyre pressure monthly when the tyres are "cold" (not driven for 3 hours)
  • Always inspect the spare tire load pressure profile regularly
  • Maintain matching pressure across matching axles for brake stabilization

Wheel Torque Specs

Tightening lug nuts to factory-recommended torque values avoids rotor warping and ensures wheels remain securely attached under stress. Over-tightening damages studs, while under-tightening causes severe vibration and potential wheel separation.

  • Use a calibrated torque wrench rather than pneumatic impact tools
  • Torque in a cross star-pattern to distribute load pressure evenly
  • Re-torque newly installed wheels after the first 100 km of driving

Rim Cleaning & Care

Brake dust, road salt, and tar cause corrosive pitting to aluminum alloy wheels if left uncleaned. Regular cleaning preserves the clear-coat protection finish, saving costly alloy rim repairs.

  • Use non-acidic cleaning agents to prevent clear-coat degradation
  • Avoid spraying cold water on hot brake rotors to prevent wrapping
  • Apply a protective wheel wax seal to repel road grime and brake dust
