The TRAX Story

Decades of passion for performance, safety, and automotive excellence.

Forged from Passion

Founded in 1998, TRAX started as a small, enthusiast-run garage dedicated to race-day track setups. We believed that true performance begins with the only part of your car that touches the ground: the tyres.

Over the years, we expanded our expertise from the track to the street, recognizing that the precision required for a race car translates perfectly into unmatched safety and longevity for daily drivers and family vehicles.

Today, we operate one of the most advanced tyre and alignment centers in the region, bringing state-of-the-art technology and old-school dedication to every customer.
