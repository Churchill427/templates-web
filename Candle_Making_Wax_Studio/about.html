Our Story

Born from a love of slow craft and quiet moments. We are a team of chandlers bringing light to your everyday rituals.

The LUME Philosophy

We believe a candle is more than just a fragrance—it's a tool for transforming a space, shifting a mood, and creating a ritual of slowness in a fast-paced world.

When Elena Torres founded LUME in 2019, she was looking for a cleaner alternative to the synthetic, overpowering candles on the market. She started melting soy wax in her Brooklyn kitchen, experimenting with essential oils until she found the perfect balance of scent throw and clean burn.

Today, every LUME candle is still hand-poured in our studio using those same principles: pure ingredients, intentional design, and artisan craftsmanship.

Our Journey

How a kitchen hobby grew into a Brooklyn institution.

2019

The Kitchen Days

Elena pours her first 100 candles in her apartment, giving them to friends and selling them at local Brooklyn craft markets.

2021

The First Studio

LUME moves out of the apartment and into a small 500 sq ft workshop in Greenpoint. We launch our signature 4-scent collection.

2023

Opening the Doors

We expand into our current studio space, opening our retail storefront and launching our first candle-making workshops.

2026

A Growing Community

Now a team of five, we pour over 2,000 candles a month and host sold-out workshops every weekend, staying true to our small-batch roots.

Our Commitment to the Earth

  • 100% US-Grown Soy: Our wax is completely renewable, carbon-neutral, and supports domestic farmers. No paraffin, no petroleum.
  • Clean Fragrance: We refuse to use phthalates, parabens, or prop 65 chemicals. Just safe, clean-burning oils.
  • Vessel Recycling Program: Bring any empty LUME vessel back to our Brooklyn studio and receive 15% off your next purchase. We clean and reuse them.
  • Plastic-Free Shipping: All our e-commerce orders ship in recycled kraft boxes with biodegradable packing peanuts.
