Crafted in Small Batches

Every detail is deliberate. Here's what sets a LUME candle apart.

01

100% Natural Soy Wax

Sourced from sustainable US farms, our soy wax burns cleaner and longer than paraffin — no toxins, no soot.

02

Cotton & Wood Wicks

We use lead-free braided cotton and crackling wood wicks, each hand-trimmed and tabbed in-studio.

03

Phthalate-Free Fragrance

Our scent blends use only clean, phthalate-free fragrance oils and pure essential oils for authentic aroma.

04

45+ Hour Burn Time

Each standard 8oz candle is engineered for a minimum 45-hour burn, so every moment lasts.

Meet the Maker

LUME began in 2019 in a small Brooklyn kitchen, when founder Elena Torres started experimenting with soy wax, essential oils, and a dream of creating candles that felt like home.

What started as a weekend hobby quickly grew into a studio, then a storefront, and now a team of five passionate artisans who share Elena's commitment to craft, sustainability, and the transformative power of scent.
