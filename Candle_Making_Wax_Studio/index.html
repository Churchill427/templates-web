The Pouring Ritual

From raw wax to radiant glow — every candle follows a four-step journey of patience and craft.

Melt

Pure soy wax gently heated to the perfect pouring temperature

Blend

Essential oils and botanicals stirred by hand for depth and character

Pour

Warm wax carefully poured into hand-selected vessels with cotton wicks

Cure

Each candle rests for 14 days to develop its full scent throw

Scent of the Month

Midnight Orchid

A sultry, evening-ready fragrance that opens with dark plum and black currant, settles into a heart of midnight orchid and velvety rose, and closes with a warm base of oud, patchouli, and vanilla bean.

Each Midnight Orchid candle is poured into a hand-thrown ceramic vessel glazed in deep aubergine, making it as much a decorative object as a sensory experience.
