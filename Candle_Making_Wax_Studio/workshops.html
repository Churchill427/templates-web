What You'll Make

Every workshop participant leaves with a hand-poured candle in their choice of vessel and scent. Our studio stocks over 30 fragrance oils and 6 vessel styles, so your creation will be truly one of a kind.

Your candle will need 24–48 hours to cure at home before its first burn. We'll send you care instructions and a LUME care card to go with it.
