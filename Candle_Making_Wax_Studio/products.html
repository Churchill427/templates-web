Candle Care Guide

A few simple habits to get the most from your LUME candle.

Trim your wick to 5mm (about 1/4 inch) before each lighting. This prevents soot, ensures an even flame height, and extends burn time. Use a wick trimmer or sharp scissors once the wax has cooled and set.
Allow the wax to melt all the way to the edge of the vessel on the first burn (usually 2–3 hours for an 8oz candle). This sets the "memory" of the candle and prevents tunnelling on future burns.
Never burn for more than 4 hours at a time. Place on a heat-resistant surface, away from drafts, curtains, and pets. Always keep the candle in sight while burning and extinguish before leaving the room.
Store candles in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and extreme temperatures. Use the lid (if included) to keep the wax dust-free and preserve the scent between burns. Soy wax has excellent scent retention when stored properly.
