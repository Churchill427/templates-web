Our Story

A journey of passion, flour, and fire — crafting memories since 2012.

Our Heritage

A Legacy of Craft

CRUM was born in 2012 from a simple belief: that honest baking can bring people together. What started as a small kitchen experiment has grown into Portland's most beloved artisan bakery.

Our founder, Marie Laurent, trained at Le Cordon Bleu before returning home with a vision — to create a bakery where every loaf, every pastry, every cake tells a story of care and craftsmanship.

Today, we serve thousands of customers each week, but our promise remains the same: real ingredients, traditional methods, and an unwavering commitment to quality.
