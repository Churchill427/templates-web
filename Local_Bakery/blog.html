Baking Tips

Quick tips from our chefs to level up your baking game.

1

Room Temperature Ingredients

Always bring butter, eggs, and dairy to room temperature before mixing for smoother batters.

2

Measure by Weight

Use a kitchen scale for precise measurements. Baking is chemistry — accuracy matters.

3

Don't Overmix

Mix until just combined. Overworking gluten makes cakes tough and breads dense.

4

Preheat Your Oven

Always preheat for at least 15 minutes. Use an oven thermometer for accuracy.

5

The Toothpick Test

Insert a toothpick in the center — it should come out with moist crumbs, not batter.

6

Cool Before Frosting

Let cakes cool completely before decorating. Warm cakes melt frosting and cause sliding.
