Our Story

From a small family workshop to a global footwear brand, our journey is built on passion, precision, and purpose.

Meet the Team

Our Mission

To make premium, handcrafted footwear accessible to everyone. We believe that exceptional shoes shouldn't be a luxury — they should be a standard. Every pair we craft blends timeless design with modern technology.

Our Vision

To lead the global footwear industry toward a sustainable future where style, quality, and responsibility walk hand in hand. By 2030, we aim for 100% sustainable materials across all product lines.

Quality Innovation Sustainability Community

Our Journey

Four decades of crafting excellence

1985

The Beginning

Founded in a small Italian workshop by master cobbler Marco Rossi Sr., driven by a passion for perfect footwear.

1995

International Expansion

Opened our first international showroom in London, bringing Italian craftsmanship to the global stage.

2005

Sustainability Commitment

Launched our first eco-friendly line, pioneering sustainable materials in premium footwear.

2015

Digital Revolution

Launched our e-commerce platform, making SOLE accessible to customers worldwide with just a click.

2020

Million Milestone

Celebrated our 1 millionth pair sold — each one crafted with the same care as the very first.

2024

Global Presence

Now available in 50+ countries with flagship stores on three continents and a thriving online community.
