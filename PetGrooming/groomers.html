Pro Tips from Our Team

Expert advice from our groomers to help you care for your pet at home

How to Brush a Long-Haired Cat

"Start from the tail and work forward, using gentle strokes. Never pull through mats — use a detangling spray first."

Maria Gonzalez

Nail Trimming Without Anxiety

"Associate the clippers with treats. Let your pet sniff them first. Trim one nail a day if needed — no rush."

David Park

Bath Time Best Practices

"Use lukewarm water and a non-slip mat. Place cotton balls in ears to prevent water entry. Always rinse thoroughly."

Sarah Mitchell

Dental Care Between Visits

"Brush teeth 2-3 times per week with pet-safe paste. Dental chews help too, but nothing replaces manual brushing."

James Chen
