Our Story

From a small grooming table in a garage to the city's most trusted pet care salon.

From Passion to Profession

Founded in 2014 by two lifelong pet lovers, Pawly started as a dream to provide grooming care that treats every pet like family. What began in a small converted garage with a single grooming table has grown into San Francisco's most beloved pet grooming destination.

Our founders, Emma Richardson and Michael Torres, both experienced the frustration of finding quality, compassionate grooming for their own pets. Rather than complain, they decided to create the solution themselves.

Today, Pawly serves over 500 pets monthly with a team of 15 certified groomers, each chosen not just for their technical skill, but for their genuine love of animals. Our philosophy is simple: every pet deserves to feel safe, comfortable, and beautiful.

Mission

To provide the highest quality grooming care in a stress-free environment, ensuring every pet leaves happy, healthy, and looking their absolute best.

Vision
