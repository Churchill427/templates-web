Common Questions

Everything you need to know before your pet's appointment

A basic bath and brush takes 45-60 minutes for small dogs, while a full grooming session with haircut and styling can take 90 minutes to 2 hours depending on your pet's size and coat condition. We never rush — your pet's comfort is our priority.

We exclusively use veterinary-approved, organic, and hypoallergenic products. Our shampoos, conditioners, and treatments are free from parabens, sulfates, and artificial fragrances. We can also accommodate pets with specific allergies or sensitivities.

No, you're welcome to drop off your pet and pick them up when the session is complete. We'll send you a text notification when your pet is ready. However, you're also welcome to watch via our live webcam feed.

Most dogs benefit from professional grooming every 4-6 weeks. Long-haired breeds may need grooming every 3-4 weeks, while short-haired breeds can go 6-8 weeks. Cats typically need grooming every 4-8 weeks depending on their coat type.

We specialize in handling anxious and nervous pets. Our groomers are trained in low-stress handling techniques, and we have private grooming suites for pets who need a calmer environment. Please let us know about any behavioral concerns when booking.
