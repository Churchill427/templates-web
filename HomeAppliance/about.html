Who We Are

Our Story & Mission

Founded in 2015, HOME bridges the gap between premium appliance sales and fast, dependable post-purchase technical care.

A Decade of Excellence

Empowering Modern Homes with Intelligent Technology

What began as a single repair workshop has grown into a premier nationwide retailer and service provider for over 50,000 satisfied households.

Our mission is simple: provide energy-efficient appliances that reduce household carbon footprints while ensuring every installation is backed by factory-trained technicians.
