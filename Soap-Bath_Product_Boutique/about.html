Our Story, Our Craft

What started as a kitchen experiment in 2016 became a passion for clean, cold-process soapmaking. Every bar carries the care of small-batch craft.

Meet the Makers

A Kitchen, A Dream, A Bar of Soap

SUDZ began in 2016 when our founder, Mira Chen, poured her first batch of cold-process soap in a tiny apartment kitchen. What started as a hobby — studying saponification, sourcing botanicals from local farmers, and gifting bars to friends — quickly became something bigger.

Friends told friends. A weekend market stall turned into a workshop. Today, every SUDZ bar is still cold-process, still hand-cut, and still cured for a full 28 days on wooden racks before it reaches your hands.

We never took shortcuts, and we never will. That patience is what makes a SUDZ bar different from anything you'll find on a shelf.

What We Stand For

Three promises we make to every customer, every bar, every day.

Clean Ingredients

Every ingredient is chosen for its benefit. No palm oil, no sulfates, no parabens — ever. We list 100% of what goes in, so you know exactly what touches your skin.

Small-Batch Craft

We pour in batches of 40. Each bar is hand-cut, stamped, and cured for 28 days. Mass production? Never. Quality and character in every single bar.

Planet-First Packaging

Recyclable kraft paper, compostable labels, zero plastic wrap. Our zero-waste pledge means every part of the package has a second life.

Our Journey

Mira poured 20 bars of lavender soap and sold out at a local farmers' market in under two hours. Something was clearly brewing.

The SUDZ Process

An interactive look at our artisanal method.

We blend pure botanicals, lye, and essential oils at precisely 110°F to start the saponification process.
The thickened trace is poured into wooden molds and insulated for 24 hours to set.
Our soaps rest in temperature-controlled racks for a minimum of 28 days to ensure a hard, long-lasting bar.
Each loaf is meticulously hand-cut into perfect 5oz bars, ready for packaging.
