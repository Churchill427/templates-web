Frequently Asked

Do you ship internationally?
Currently, we only ship within the US and Canada. We hope to expand our shipping zones next year!
Are your products vegan?
95% of our products are vegan. A few select bars contain raw honey or beeswax, which are clearly marked on the ingredient list.
How long does a bar of soap last?
With daily use, our bars typically last 3-4 weeks. To extend their life, always keep them on a well-draining soap dish and let them dry completely between uses.
