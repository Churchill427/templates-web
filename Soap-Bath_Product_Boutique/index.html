The Craft Process

From raw botanicals to your bathroom shelf — four steps, twenty-eight days, zero shortcuts.

Nourish

Select premium oils — olive, coconut, shea — cold-pressed and unrefined.

Blend

Fold in botanicals, clays, and essential oils at precisely the right trace.

Pour

Hand-pour into wooden molds, swirl the design, insulate to set overnight.

Cure

Rest for 28 days on curing racks. Patience makes every bar gentler.
