EXPERT GROOMING GUIDES

Master barber advice to maintain your fresh look between salon visits

Beard Maintenance

Daily Beard Hydration Protocol

Apply 3-4 drops of organic jojoba & argan beard oil immediately after warm shower when pores are open. Comb gently downward to prevent ingrown hairs and distribute natural oils.

Scalp Health

Scalp Balance & Hair Density

Avoid washing hair daily with harsh sulfates. Use a cool-water rinse on non-wash days and massage tea tree scalp tonic twice weekly to stimulate healthy hair follicle growth.

Fade Upkeep

Maintaining Skin Fades & Line-Ups

Schedule 14-day neck line cleanup sessions. Keep temple edges moisturized with aloe vera lotion to eliminate razor bumps and maintain razor-sharp contrast.

Shave Care

Hot Towel Shave Pre & Post Care

Always shave in the direction of hair growth. Finish with an alcohol-free witch hazel splash and cold towel press to close pores and lock in skin moisture.
