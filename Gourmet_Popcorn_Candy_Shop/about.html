Our Story Starts with a Kernel

From a home kitchen to a beloved artisan brand — meet the people and passion behind every POPZ creation.

From One Kettle to Thousands of Smiles

In 2014, Elena Rodriguez started popping kernels in a secondhand copper kettle in her grandmother's kitchen. What began as a weekend hobby — making caramel popcorn for neighbors — quickly grew into a local sensation at farmers' markets and craft fairs.

By 2016, POPZ had its own storefront on Confection Lane in Sweet Haven, California. Today, our team of twelve artisan confectioners handcrafts over 40 unique flavors, ships across the country, and supplies hundreds of events each year — all while staying true to the small-batch, quality-first philosophy that started it all.

Every tin that leaves our workshop carries Elena's original promise: "No shortcuts, no fillers — just real ingredients and real flavor."

What We Stand For

Three principles guide everything we do — from sourcing to shipping.

Artisan Craft

Every batch is hand-popped, hand-coated, and hand-inspected. We never mass-produce — each tin gets personal attention.

Premium Quality

Non-GMO kernels, real butter, single-origin cocoa, and locally sourced fruits. We use ingredients we'd serve our own families.

Community First

10% of seasonal profits go to local food banks. We sponsor school events and partner with neighborhood businesses.

Our Journey

Key milestones on the road from kitchen experiment to community staple.

2014

The First Batch

Elena pops the first batch of Caramel Gold in her grandmother's kitchen and starts selling at the local farmers' market.

2016

742 Confection Lane

POPZ opens its first storefront in Sweet Haven, California, with three signature flavors and one very busy copper kettle.

2019

Nationwide Shipping

Online store launches. Custom gift tin builder debuts. Orders start arriving from all 50 states.

2022

40+ Flavors

The flavor catalog hits 40 varieties. Corporate gifting program launches, partnering with Fortune 500 companies.

2026

Today & Beyond

Over 125,000 tins shipped, 12 team members, and a new seasonal collection every quarter. The best is yet to come.
