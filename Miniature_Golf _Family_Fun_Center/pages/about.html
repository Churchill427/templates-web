Our Story

Built by parents, golfers, and dreamers to bring world-class entertainment, friendly competition, and pure celebration to families across the region.

Since 2016

A Decade of Smiles & Strikes

PUTT began with a simple dream: create an immersive entertainment haven where adults have just as much fun as kids. We started with two handcrafted mini-golf courses inspired by global landscapes and quickly expanded to include high-tech arcades, electric go-karts, and boutique party suites.

Today, PUTT welcomes over 50,000 visitors every year, offering safe, sparkling clean, and environmentally conscious family leisure.
