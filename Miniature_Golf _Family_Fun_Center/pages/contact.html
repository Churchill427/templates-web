Getting Here

Transit & Parking Options

Easy access whether you are driving, taking public transit, or cycling.

By Car

Take Exit 42 from Route 101 South toward Fairway Parkway. PUTT is 2 minutes on your right opposite Green Meadow Park.

By Bus & Light Rail

Metro Rapid Lines 14 and 88 stop directly in front of PUTT at Fairway & Pine Station, running every 12 minutes.

Free On-Site Parking

Over 250 complimentary parking spots including wide stroller-accessible spaces and 8 Level-2 EV charging bays.

Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Contact Questions

What is the best way to book groups larger than 20?
For groups of 20 or more, we recommend filling out the contact form above with the subject "School / Group Rates" or calling our group events team at (555) PUTT-FUN for bulk pricing and private pavilion booking.
Are service animals allowed at PUTT?
Yes, ADA-defined trained service animals are warmly welcomed throughout our miniature golf courses, snack bar, and arcade zones.
Where is the lost and found desk located?
Lost items are secured at the Main Pro Desk at our entrance. You can also call us with an item description and our security staff will check immediately.

Ready to Play?

Reserve your preferred tee time today or drop in anytime. We cannot wait to see your family on the green!
