Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about ticket validation, refunds, age requirements, and equipment.

Do I need to book in advance or can I walk in?
Walk-ins are always welcomed! However, during peak hours (Friday evenings and weekends), online reservations receive guaranteed priority tee-times and avoid waiting.
Do pass credits expire?
Never! Any arcade points or unused pass credits on your PUTT Fun Pass card stay valid indefinitely and can be used on any future visit.
What is the rain or weather policy?
Two of our four courses are completely indoor and climate-controlled, and our arcade and go-kart track are 100% indoors. In the event of severe weather, outdoor tee-times can be transferred indoors or rescheduled without fee.
Are putters and balls provided with admission?
Yes, all putters (available in sizes from toddler to tall adult) and regulation golf balls are included in your ticket price. You can also pick your favorite ball color at the pro desk!
Can we bring outside food and birthday cakes?
Outside cakes and cupcakes are permitted for guests who have booked an official Birthday Party Package. Other outside food is not permitted, as our Snack Bar offers pizzas, burgers, chicken tenders, and healthy options.
